Our Story

Built By Someone Who
Felt This Pain Firsthand

Elite Remote Agent was born out of frustration. As a real estate agent and business owner, our founder James Burbank spent years watching talented professionals drown in admin work — following up leads at midnight, missing transactions because nobody was coordinating, and paying $50,000+ for US staff when the work could be done better, faster, and more reliably by a trained remote professional.

After building and running his own VA operations through Divine Growth Remote Agent VA, James saw the gap — there was no platform that combined genuine pre-screening, real estate specific training, and a simple subscription model that made hiring feel as easy as ordering from Amazon.

So we built it. Elite Remote Agent is the platform we wished existed — and we built it for agents, team leaders, and business owners who are serious about scaling.
