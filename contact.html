Quick Answers
Common Questions Before the Demo
Is the demo really free with no obligation? ▼
Yes. 100% free. No credit card. No pressure. It's a 20-minute conversation — if ERA isn't right for you, we'll tell you that honestly.
Can I hire a VA the same day as the demo? ▼
Absolutely. If you subscribe during or right after the demo, you can be browsing VAs within minutes. Many clients hire their first VA the same day they discover the platform.
What if I'm not sure which VA role I need? ▼
That's exactly what the demo is for. We ask you about your business and tell you specifically which VA role will make the biggest impact. Most people leave with total clarity.
Do you work with non-real estate businesses? ▼
Yes. Our Business Portal serves med spas, law firms, insurance agencies, mortgage companies, e-commerce stores, and more. We have industry-trained VAs for most business types.
How long does it take to get my first VA working? ▼
Most clients have their first VA hired and onboarded within 48 hours of subscribing. Some do it in the same day. The platform is built to move fast.
What if the demo time slots don't work for me? ▼
Just send us a message with your preferred time and we'll make it work. We're flexible and can accommodate evenings and weekends for busy professionals.
