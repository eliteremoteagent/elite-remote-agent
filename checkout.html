Common Questions
What happens right after I pay? ▼
You get immediate access to the full platform — browse VA profiles, listen to voice recordings, view resumes, and hire your first VA the same day. No waiting period. For Starter, your $49/mo billing begins automatically 30 days after your one-time $149 setup fee — you won't need to re-enter payment details.
What's the actual difference between plans? ▼
All plans include unlimited browsing, hiring, and replacements. The difference is how much our team does for you. Starter = you run it yourself. Growth = we help you set up, train your VAs, and check in monthly. Elite = we run your entire VA operation through a dedicated account manager.
Can I really cancel anytime? ▼
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You can cancel from your account dashboard at any time. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.
How does annual billing work? ▼
For Growth and Elite, annual billing charges your card once for the full year at a discounted rate — equivalent to getting 2 months completely free. You save roughly 17% vs monthly billing. Starter is monthly-only.
Is there a limit on how many VAs I can hire? ▼
Zero limits on all plans. Browse as many as you want. Hire as many as you need. Replace any that aren't working. Your subscription gives you full platform access — not a per-hire fee model.
What if my VA isn't a good fit? ▼
No problem. All plans include unlimited replacements. If a VA isn't working out for any reason, browse the platform and hire a new one immediately. No extra cost, no questions asked.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? ▼
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Is my payment information secure? ▼
Completely. We use Stripe for all payment processing — the same technology used by Amazon, Shopify, and millions of other businesses. We never store your card details on our servers.
